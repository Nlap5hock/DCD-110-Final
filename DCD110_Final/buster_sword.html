Buster Sword

The Buster Sword is one of the most iconic weapons in the Final Fantasy series, and gaming as a whole.
Wielded by Final Fantasy VII's protaganist, Cloud Strife, the Buster Sword is the first weapon used
by the character, which can later be swapped out for stronger ones.

In-universe, the Buster Sword was first wielded by Cloud's friend and fellow SOLDIER, Zack Fair,
before being passed on to Cloud. The sword iteself has had many itereations throughout the series,
like the Fusion Sword in FFV: Advent Children and the bandaged-up look from Kingdom Hearts.
