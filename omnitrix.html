Omnitrix

The Omnitrix is a watch-shaped artifact that comes
from the world of Ben-10, and is used by the main
character, Ben Tennyson. It allows Ben to transform
into different super-powered species of aliens,
using the alien DNA stored within it.

Ben Tennyson found the Omnitrix during a summer road trip with his Grandpa Max and cousin Gwen.
While camping in the woods, Ben saw a meteor crashing nearby. When he went to investigate, he
saw that it was really a space pod containing the Omnitrix. The device immediately latched
onto his wrist and bonded with him, giving him the ability to transform into different alien
heroes. This moment began Ben’s journey as a young hero in the Ben 10 universe.
