Smash Ball

The Smash Ball is one of the most powerful items in the Super Smash Brothers series of games.
It first became an item in the series' third entry, Super Smash Bros. Brawl, and allows the
fighter to use their super-move, called a "Final Smash". The item itself doesn't have a
specific in-universe origin, but was designed using the game's logo to show its importance.
