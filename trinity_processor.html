Trinity Processor

The Trinity Proccessor is one of the most powerful artifacts
from the Xenoblade Chronicles series and comes from Xenoblade Chronicles 2.
It was designed to manage the Conduit, a mysterious source of limitless energy.
The system was divided into three core processing units: Ontos, Logos, and Pneuma,
each with its own intelligence and purpose. After an experiment, the Trinity
Processor became tied to the creation of new worlds and the fate of the countless
lives within them.
