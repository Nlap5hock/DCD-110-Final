The One Ring

The One Ring was formed by The Lord of the Rings himself, the dark lord Sauron.
The ring went through may hands after Sauron's defeat but the most well-known
users of the ring are Gollem, the hobbit Bilbo Baggins, and his nephew
Frodo Baggins. Though it granted power and invisibility, the Ring also
slowly consumed the minds of its owners. In the end, it became
the center of a great quest to destroy it once and for all.
