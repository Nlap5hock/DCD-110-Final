Master Sword

The Master Sword is the main weapon in the Legend of Zelda series and an icon of gaming.
The blade itself has gone through a few different incarnations over the years, but its design
has stayed largely the same.

The Master Sword is wielded by Link, the main protagonist of The Legend of Zelda series.
Although there are actually many different "Links" throughout the series,
usually a each game has it's own Link who decended from another
Link who was a hero in the Kingdom of Hyrule's past. The Master Sword
itself doesn't change, though. No matter how many
heroes have fought with it, it has been the same blade over
the millenia the series takes place.
