Blue Beetle's Scarab

Blue Bettle is a hero from the DC Universe powered by the Scarab. The scarab that
bonded to Blue Beetle, known as Khaji-Da, is actually ancient alien technology
created by the The Reach, a race that conquers planets through infiltration rather
than open war. The Reach would secretly leave scarabs on different planets, where
they bonded to chosen hosts and turned them into powerful living weapons with armor,
flight, energy blasts, and a near endless arsenal of other weapons. Khaji-Da was
meant to control its host completely, but when it bonded with Jaime Reyes, the
Blue Beetle, something malfunctioned, allowing Jaime to keep his free will.
