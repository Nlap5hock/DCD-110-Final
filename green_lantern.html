Green Lantern Ring

Appearing in the DC universe, the Green Lantern Ring is a powerful weapon wielded by members of the Green Lantern Corps.
The ring grants its wearer incredible abilities, including flight, energy projection, and the power to create constructs
out of green, hard-light energy.

















Though there are many who use the power of green lantern rings,
the most notable are Hal Jordan, Jon Stewart, Guy Gardener,
Kyle Rayner, and Kilowag.
